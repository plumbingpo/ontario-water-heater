Keeping Your Pomona Water Heater Running Longer

Many premature tank failures trace back to maintenance that never happened, and the habits that prevent them are simple. Drain and flush the tank once a year to clear sediment, get the anode rod checked every few years, and test the relief valve annually so it doesn't seize. Set the thermostat close to 120 degrees to reduce scald risk and slow scale buildup. If you have a tankless unit, descale it yearly where the water is hard to protect the heat exchanger.

How old is your water heater? The rating plate on the tank usually includes the manufacture date, and its age tells you a lot. Most tank models make it to 8 to 12 years, heat pump units 10 to 15, and well-maintained tankless units around 20. If your heater is past that range and is also showing discolored water, rumbling, or moisture at the base, it's usually due for a planned replacement, and that's almost always cheaper and easier on everyone than an emergency replacement after a leak.

Small problems rarely stay small when they're ignored. Consider a spent anode rod: swapping it out is a short, inexpensive repair, but once it's gone, the water begins corroding the tank steel, and just like that a minor fix becomes a full replacement. That's why we encourage homeowners to save our number in their phone and reach out at the first hint of trouble. Catching it early saves time and money and prevents a flooded utility room.

Hot Water Headaches Pomona Homeowners Face

A water heater in Pomona rarely fails without warning. Watch for rumbling and popping during heating, rust-colored hot water, a faint rotten-egg smell, a damp spot under the tank, or showers that run cold sooner every month. Those point to sediment buildup, a spent anode rod, a failing element, or corrosion starting at the bottom seam, and caught early, most of them mean a modest repair or a replacement on your schedule. Ignored, they lead to a ruptured tank and a cleanup bill. A dripping T&P valve deserves special attention: it's the tank telling you pressure or temperature is running too high, and it needs a professional look soon. We find the root cause instead of treating symptoms, so the small problem never gets the chance to grow.
