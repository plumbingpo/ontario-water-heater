Safety

Installation, Inspection, and Record-Keeping

A poorly installed or neglected water heater can lead to carbon monoxide hazards, scalding, bacterial growth in lukewarm storage, and costly water damage from a ruptured tank. That's a real liability for a commercial property owner with employees, customers, and tenants in the building. Every installation and inspection we perform covers venting, combustion air, gas line sizing, relief valve discharge, expansion tanks, mixing valves, and code items like drain pans and seismic straps where required, followed by a written report that ranks each risk by priority and recommends specific fixes.

This work serves a dual purpose: it protects people and property, and it provides the documentation that demonstrates due diligence to landlords, insurers, and health or building inspectors. We maintain detailed service logs, temperature records, relief valve test dates, and a documentation binder for every account, with more frequent service for heavy-use equipment in kitchens, laundries, and large mechanical rooms.

Rapid Response

Rapid-Response Water Heater Repair for Businesses

A hot water failure on a commercial property can shut down a kitchen, cancel a day of salon appointments, leave hotel guests with cold showers, and spark tenant disputes. Ontario Water Heater provides fast emergency water heater service for commercial properties throughout Ontario and surrounding areas, and we schedule non-urgent work after hours or before opening to limit downtime.

No two commercial emergencies look the same. It might be a failed gas valve, a burned-out element, a flame sensor that stopped proving flame, a tripped high-limit switch, a leaking tank, a stalled recirculation pump, or a tankless bank throwing error codes. Whatever it is, we diagnose it fast, restore hot water, and record every step for your facility files. Afterward, we put maintenance in place so the same failure doesn't return.

Ongoing Protection

Commercial Water Heater Maintenance Programs

The most cost-effective way to manage commercial water heaters is preventing failures before they happen. Ontario Water Heater offers customized maintenance programs for commercial properties of all types and sizes. Our programs include scheduled inspections, tank flushing, anode rod checks, tankless descaling, relief valve testing, burner and venting inspection, recirculation pump and mixing valve checks, and temperature verification at the fixtures. Each program is tailored to the property's equipment, water quality, usage patterns, and budget.
